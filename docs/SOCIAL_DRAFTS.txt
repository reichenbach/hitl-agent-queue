Most multi-agent demos are chat relays. That is how you get silent stalls and three tools rewriting the same file.

I open-sourced a small reference for the pattern I actually want in an AI workflow seat: a durable ticket board, role claims, a human gate on irreversible steps, and short progress pings when a stage completes.

Cursor (or any draft worker) claims a ticket and attaches materials. You approve from your phone with a one-line grammar: APPROVE TKT-0001 CLAUDE or SKIP. Codex or Claude picks up a frozen packet. Silence means stuck, not success.

Repo: https://github.com/reichenbach/hitl-agent-queue

Synthetic demo only. Stdlib Python. Optional Cortland iMessage for the notify path.

If you run internal automations with a human stop you can trust, this is the shape.
